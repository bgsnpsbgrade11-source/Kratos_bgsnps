AGE CATEGORIES

U-14

U-17

REGISTRATION FEE

₹2,000

RULES

  • Team Composition: Each team must consist of 12 players (5 on-court players + 7 substitutes).
  • Governing Rules: The tournament will be conducted as per the latest rules and regulations of the Basketball Federation of India (BFI).
  • Tournament Format: All matches will be played on a knockout basis.
  • Match Duration: Preliminary Rounds: 4 quarters of 7 minutes each. Semi-finals & Finals: 4 quarters of 10 minutes each.
  • Referee's Decision: The referee's decision will be final. No protests will be entertained.
  • Code of Conduct: Teams indulging in misbehaviour or misconduct on the court will be disqualified from the tournament.
  • Reporting Time: Teams must report at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match time.
  • Multiple Matches: Teams should be prepared to play up to 4 matches in a day. Either the committee reserves the right to postpone and alter the dates and order of the matches.
  • Final Authority: The decision of the Tournament Committee will be final. The committee comprises the Principal, Referees, and the Department of Physical Education of the BGS NATIONAL PUBLIC SCHOOL, SAKALAVARA
