IMPORTANT INFORMATION

GENERAL
INSTRUCTIONS.

All students representing their respective schools at the *BGSNPS Sakalavara Kratos Inter-School Sports Fest 2026–27* are required to strictly adhere to the following instructions

01

PARTICIPATION

Eligibility & Registration

  • Participants must be bona fide students of the registering school.
  • Every participating student must carry a *valid School Identity Card* and present it upon arrival for verification.
  • Age categories must be strictly followed. Any violation will result in disqualification.
  • Registration fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Registration details cannot be casually modified after final submission.
02

CONDUCT

Code of Conduct

  • All participants, teachers, and spectators must maintain discipline and sportsmanship at all times.
  • Any form of misbehaviour or misconduct will result in immediate disqualification.
  • The decision of the referees and the tournament committee shall be final and binding.
  • No protests will be entertained regarding referee decisions.
  • Teams must report at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match time.
  • Proper sports attire and equipment are mandatory for all events.
  • Students are requested *not to carry or consume food in the playing areas*. Food may be consumed only in the designated eating areas.
  • Students are advised *not to bring valuable or expensive personal belongings* to the event. The organising school will *not be responsible for any loss, theft, or damage* to personal belongings.
03

VENUE & SCHEDULE

Venue & Reporting

  • Venue: BGS National Public School, Sakalavara, Bannerghatta, Bengaluru - 560083.
  • All participating school teams must report to the designated venue *by 7:30 AM sharp* for registration. Late arrival may affect participation in the events.
  • Teams should be prepared to play multiple matches in a day.
04

PAYMENT

Registration Fee & Payment

  • The total registration fee is calculated based on the selected events.
  • Payment must be made via UPI using the QR code on the payment page.
  • After completing payment, keep your transaction ID or payment screenshot for reference.
05

AWARDS

Prizes & Recognition

  • Trophies, medals, and certificates will be awarded to winners and runners-up.
  • Participation certificates will be provided to all participants.
  • Prize distribution will be held as per the event schedule.
